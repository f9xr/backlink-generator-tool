Before you send a report

  • Confirm the URL is entered in full, including the path and query string.
  • Try the run again with IFrame mode and concurrency five.
  • Check whether your browser blocked pop-ups, frames or the download.
  • Note the exact row that failed rather than the whole list.

Most cross results are rate limits or frame-blocked targets, and they change from day to day. A target that fails once may load on a later run. A target that fails every time is worth reporting.

Send a report

The form below opens your email app with the details already filled in. Nothing is submitted to a server, because this site has no backend.

By sending a report you agree that we may quote the technical details to fix the issue. We never publish your email address.

Response times

Reports are read in the order they arrive. Broken or unsafe targets are prioritised because they affect every visitor. Expect a reply within a few business days; if your report is about a security issue, say so in the subject line.

What happens after a report

  • We reproduce the problem with the URL and mode you sent.
  • If a template is dead or unsafe, it is removed from the JSON list and the change ships with the next deploy.
  • If the problem is in the UI, it is tracked as a fix and noted in the release notes.
  • If we cannot reproduce it, we ask for your browser details and try again.
