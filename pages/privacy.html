Privacy policy

The short version: this tool has no backend and no analytics. It stores one preference cookie and makes only the requests needed to run a submission.

What we collect

We do not run a server that receives your URLs, and we do not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts on this site. The generator reads the URL you type, builds links in memory and opens them from your browser.

If you email us, we receive the address and contents of your message and keep it only as long as needed to answer.

Cookies and local storage

The tool sets one first-party cookie named bg. It stores your selected mode, concurrency, batch limit and checkbox preferences so the next visit remembers them. It contains no URL you generated and no identifier that follows you across sites. Clearing cookies removes it.

Third-party requests

Running the tool necessarily contacts third-party sites. Those requests include:

  • The generated backlink, ping, share and archive URLs, which receive your target URL according to each service's own behaviour.
  • An optional thum.io screenshot preview of the URL you typed, shown in the tool.
  • An optional Wayback Machine submission when you tick that box.
  • The public CORS proxy listed in the project data when you use Ping mode.
  • The F9XR articles sitemap, fetched to show the latest posts. If your browser blocks it, the shipped list is used.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy. We do not control what they log.

Advertising

This site is prepared for Google AdSense but does not currently load advertising scripts. If ads are enabled later, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads. This policy will be updated before any ad script goes live, and consent controls will be provided where required.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data from the tool, there is usually nothing to export or delete. If you emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct or object to processing of your personal data.

Children

The tool is intended for professional and general audience use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Contact

Questions about this page can be sent to hello@f9xr.org. We answer from the F9XR Team, the operator of backlink.is-an.ai.
