    Overview: a true “viral” backlink maker

    This online SEO tool runs entirely in your browser (no servers). Paste your URL, click Generate Backlinks, and it fires curated endpoints that help bots find and revisit your page. Every action is tracked with pending, success, or failure. When you’re done, click Download URLs to export your run log. Because it’s client-side, it’s private, fast, and easy to re-run whenever you update content.

    Heads-up: Popup/Tab modes create visible, top-level loads. If nothing opens, your browser blocked pop-ups. Enable pop-ups for this site to capture stronger top-level visit signals.

    Modes explained (IFrame • Popup • Tab • Ping)

    • IFrame - Silent, high-volume background loads. Great for continuous “index booster” sweeps.
    • Popup - Opens windows (allow pop-ups) for sites that register only foreground top-level visits.
    • Tab - Similar to Popup, with an option to reuse a single tab for cleaner window management.
    • Ping - Lightweight URL pinger calls to kick crawlers and archives for fast discovery.

    Why it improves SEO

    Search engines find content via links, pings, and snapshots. By distributing your URL across archives, sharers, and ping endpoints, you multiply crawl paths, refresh freshness signals, and accelerate instant indexing opportunities. For videos, consistent YouTube backlinks can help you rank YouTube videos faster and amplify external clicks from descriptions, comments, and embeds.

    Power controls that matter

    • Max Concurrent - control speed vs. stability; start at 5, move up as your device allows.
    • Reuse window/tab - keep Popup/Tab tidy while still delivering top-level navigations.
    • Shuffle templates - randomize sequence to avoid repetitive patterns across reruns.
    • Repeat after completion - loop a set while you work on on-page tweaks.
